Medical Disclaimer

Last updated: July 2026

If your baby has a fever, is vomiting forcefully, has blood in their stool, seems limp or unusually difficult to wake, or you are ever seriously worried about their health, contact your pediatrician or emergency services immediately. Do not wait on anything from this site.

Colic Protocol, including the free 90-second assessment, the free checklist, this website, and the Calm Baby Blueprint, provides educational information about common patterns associated with infant colic. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis, and not a substitute for care from a licensed pediatrician or other qualified health provider.

About the founder's credential

Colic Protocol was built by an epidemiologist, someone trained to read, weight, and synthesize population-level research literature. That training supports evaluating and explaining published studies clearly. It is not a pediatric, gastroenterology, or lactation credential, and nothing on this site should be read as clinical advice specific to your baby.

The free assessment is a screening tool, not a diagnosis

The assessment identifies which of three common patterns, gut, nervous system, or acoustic, most closely matches the answers you provide. It is a starting point for further reading and discussion with your pediatrician, not a clinical diagnosis of your baby's condition.

Always consult your pediatrician before

  • Starting any new supplement, including probiotics, for your baby
  • Making significant changes to feeding method or diet
  • If your baby's crying pattern changes suddenly or is accompanied by any of the warning signs above

Results vary

Every baby's colic has its own combination of contributing factors. The Blueprint's 72-hour guarantee reflects our confidence in the approach, it is not a guarantee of a specific outcome for every baby.
