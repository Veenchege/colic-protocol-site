Safety-Critical Screening

When to Call the Doctor Right Away

Colic is normal crying in a healthy, growing infant. Stop and seek medical care if you observe any of these warning signs:

• Fever under 3 months: 100.4°F (38°C) or higher.
• Repeated forceful vomiting: Projectile vomiting, or green/yellow bile.
• Blood in stool or vomit: Any visible blood or dark flecks.
• Dehydration signs: Fewer than 6 wet diapers in 24 hours, dry lips, sunken fontanelle.
• Lethargy or weak cry: Very hard to wake, floppy, or unusual high-pitched cry.
Disclaimer: This app is educational and does not provide clinical diagnoses. Always consult your pediatrician before altering infant care.
