Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information Colic Protocol collects when you use colicprotocol.baby, why we collect it, and who we share it with. If anything here is unclear, email hello@colicprotocol.baby and we'll explain it plainly.

What we collect

Assessment and checklist. When you take the free assessment or use the free checklist, we collect your first name, email address, your baby's age in weeks, how your baby is fed, your answers, and which of three starting-point patterns (gut, nervous system, or feeding mechanics) your answers pointed to. These patterns are a place to start, not a diagnosis.

Safety check. Before showing results or the tracker, we ask whether your baby has any warning signs, such as fever or blood in the stool. In the assessment, your answer is saved with your results. In the tracker, it stays on your device unless you turn on online saving. This is health-related information about your baby, so we treat it with extra care.

Tracker and tonight tools. If you use the Bloom tracker or the Midnight Protocol, we collect what you log: feeds, sleep, diapers, crying episodes, what you tried and whether it helped, and the times. You can also give your baby's first name, which is optional. Please do not enter a surname or any other identifying detail.

A random device ID. The tracker stores a random ID and your log in your browser's local storage (not a cookie) so your log stays with you. Your log stays on your device unless you choose to save it online in the tracker's settings, which is off by default. If you do, it is copied to our database so it can be restored on another device. Until you give us an email, the log is not linked to your name.

Purchases. When you buy the Calm Baby Blueprint, payment is processed entirely by Gumroad. We never see or store your card details.

Site traffic. Cloudflare Web Analytics provides aggregate, cookieless traffic data. We also use Google Analytics 4, which collects pages visited, session duration, and general device and location information, and uses cookies (_ga, _ga_*) to tell visitors apart. IP addresses are anonymised by default. We do not use Meta Pixel or any advertising retargeting pixel on this site.

How we use it

  • To send you your assessment result and the free tools you asked for
  • To show you patterns in your own log and to pre-fill assessment answers your log can answer, which you can always change
  • To write to you about your baby by name, if you gave a name
  • To restore your log on another device when you verify your email with a one-time code
  • To send a short email sequence relevant to your baby's likely starting pattern, and occasionally to tell you about the Calm Baby Blueprint or related updates
  • To show you information about the Blueprint at moments when your log suggests it may help, for example when your log shows no clear drop in crying. You can ignore or dismiss it
  • To work out which platform referred you, using UTM parameters, so we know which content is helpful, not to track you individually elsewhere

We do not use your log or your baby's details for advertising, and we do not sell them.

Who we share it with

We use MailerLite to send email and store your name, your baby's name, your email, and your tags. We use Supabase to store assessment records and your tracker log and to send one-time codes to your email. We use Gumroad to process Blueprint purchases and deliver the download. We use Cloudflare to host this site and run its backend functions. These providers may process data in the United States and other countries. None of them is permitted to sell your information, and we don't sell it either, to anyone, ever.

How long we keep it

We keep your log and profile until you delete them or ask us to. Assessment and email records are kept until you unsubscribe and ask us to delete them.

Your choices

  • Delete your log: turn off online saving in the tracker's settings, or use "Delete all my data". This removes your log and profile from your device and from our database. It does not remove your email subscription or assessment record, so email us if you want those deleted too.
  • Export your log: use "Export my log as CSV" in the tracker.
  • Unsubscribe: every email includes an unsubscribe link.
  • Access, correct or delete anything else: email hello@colicprotocol.baby. Depending on where you live, such as the UK, the EU or some US states, you may have additional legal rights over your data, and we will honour them.

Children's privacy

This site is for adult parents and caregivers, not for children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. The details you give about your baby are provided by you as their parent or caregiver, and you can have them deleted at any time.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how we use your information, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, for material changes, email active subscribers directly.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: hello@colicprotocol.baby
